BOUNDARY NUMERICS

Transformed coordinates within 0.000001 metre of an exact V2 grid boundary are
snapped to that boundary before binding. The projection audit records whether X
or Y was snapped. This prevents floating-point reprojection residue from silently
turning an exact shared boundary into a false INTERIOR classification.

The policy does not convert approximate coordinates into exact coordinates.
Coordinate_Evidence_State=VERIFIED_SOURCE_COORDINATE and
Uncertainty_Class=EXACT remain mandatory.

FAILURE HANDLING

Missing or malformed Source_CRS must fail.
Source_CRS must be an explicit EPSG:<code>.
A non-EPSG free-form PROJ/WKT string must fail.
Coordinate_Evidence_State other than VERIFIED_SOURCE_COORDINATE must fail.
Uncertainty_Class other than EXACT must fail.
Non-finite coordinates must fail.
Unavailable or non-best CRS transformation must fail.
Duplicate Repository/Record_ID identities must fail.
EDGE_SHARED must not be collapsed to one Cell_ID.
CORNER_SHARED must not be collapsed to one Cell_ID.
OUTSIDE must not fabricate a Cell_ID.
Do not add consumer-side coordinate transformation or coordinate-to-cell computation.

PROVENANCE RULE

A successful CRS transform and V2 binding proves only deterministic spatial
resolution of the supplied source coordinate. It does not independently certify
that the source coordinate describes the real-world record correctly. That
evidentiary predicate is represented by VERIFIED_SOURCE_COORDINATE and must be
established upstream from source evidence.

Migration provenance for this slice:
RECOMPUTED_FROM_SOURCE_COORDINATE

Non-exact and V1-only records remain UNMIGRATED until a separate evidence-authorized vector.
