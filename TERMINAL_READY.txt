BOUNDARY NUMERICS

Transformed coordinates within 0.000001 metre of an exact V2 grid boundary are
snapped to that boundary before binding. The projection audit records whether X
or Y was snapped. This prevents floating-point reprojection residue from silently
turning an exact shared boundary into a false INTERIOR classification.

The policy does not convert approximate coordinates into exact coordinates.
Coordinate_Evidence_State=VERIFIED_SOURCE_COORDINATE and
Uncertainty_Class=EXACT remain mandatory.

GEOMETRY SEMANTICS

Polygon touch-only contact does not produce a binding row.
Polygon bindings require strictly positive overlap area.
Line bindings require strictly positive in-cell length.
A line exactly coincident with an internal grid boundary preserves both shared
cells; it is not silently assigned to one side.
The frozen binding schema is unchanged. Binding_Method and Geometry_State carry
the non-point predicate semantics; canonical geometry remains Spiderweb-owned.

LINE LENGTH SEMANTICS (THIS BUILD)
Clipped_Traversal_Length_m measures traversed length including repeated passes.
Distinct_Clipped_Spatial_Support_Length_m dissolves overlapping source
segments and measures unique geometric support (not number of visits).
Retraversed_Spatial_Length_m = traversed length - distinct support length.
Cell_Bound_Length_Sum_m sums all qualifying per-cell line segments.
Grid_Edge_Length_Multiplicity_m = cell-bound sum - traversed length;
it measures multiple cell assignments due to shared grid boundaries,
NOT repeated source traversals. Never interpret either as feature identity.
