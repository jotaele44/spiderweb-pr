PURPOSE

Two independent registries of candidate geographic features on one shared
evidence foundation. "ILAP" and "OSAP" already name other things in this
repository; the registries do not use those acronyms as identifiers. See
docs/ACCESS_POINT_TERMINOLOGY_DECISION.md.

WHAT A PASSING RUN MEANS

The software behaves as specified on synthetic controls and on the committed
registry documents. It does not mean any record is a real access point, that
any method has been validated on real terrain or seabed, or that anything is
ready for production.

REAL-SURVEY PROCESSING

   Re-check the committed run offline (needs the 'dem' extra for GeoTIFF reading):
   python scripts/process_access_point_survey.py --run-id 2026-11-17 --check

   Fetching a product needs its own grant, which bounds files and bytes:
   SPIDERWEB_ACCESS_POINT_ACQUISITION="product:<files>:<bytes>:<who authorised>"
   Products are fetched by exact address only (spiderweb/access_points/products.py)
   and are kept outside the repository; only their hashes and a small window are
   committed. Runs are declared in configs/access_point_survey_runs.yaml.
